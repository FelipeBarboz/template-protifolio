Visual identity for Paris
fashion house Loewe

Our approach to the visual identity was rooted in the brand's heritage, carefully balancing traditional craftsmanship with contemporary minimalism. We explored typographic nuances that reflect the house's evolution, creating a system that feels both timeless and inherently modern.

The resulting design language establishes a quiet authority, utilizing a monochromatic palette and precise grid structures to allow the collections to remain the focal point. This foundational work extends across all touchpoints, from packaging to digital environments.
