Golden Hour

CLIENT VOGUE
YEAR 2023

An exploration of natural light and its interaction with brutalist forms. This project aimed to capture the ephemeral moments when the setting sun transforms cold concrete into a canvas of warm hues and dramatic geometries. The strict minimalist approach allows the interplay of shadow and light to become the primary subject of the visual narrative.
