Golden Hour

A cinematic exploration of light and shadow, captured during the transition between day and night. This project examines the ephemeral nature of architectural spaces when stripped of artificial illumination, relying solely on the descending sun to define form and volume.

Through a series of carefully choreographed visual sequences, Golden Hour challenges the viewer's perception of permanence, presenting iconic structures as transient reflections of atmospheric conditions.
